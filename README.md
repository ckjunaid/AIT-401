Module Breakdown

Module I: Deep vs. Shallow Networks, Perceptron & Training Rule, Activation Functions, Loss Functions, Backpropagation, Overfitting, Underfitting & Bias-Variance Tradeoff

Module II: Gradient Descent Variants & Optimizers (Momentum, NAG, RMSProp, Adam), Vanishing & Exploding Gradients, Weight Initialization (Xavier & Kaiming), Regularization (L1/L2, Dropout, Early Stopping, Batch Norm, Data Augmentation), Ensemble Methods

Module III: CNN Architecture & Stages, Stride, Padding, Pooling & Output Dimension Math, Convolution Variants & Kernel Flipping, Parameter Sharing, Equivariance, Sparse Interactions, Pre-trained CNNs (AlexNet, ZFNet, VGG-19, ResNet-50) & Transfer Learning

Module IV: RNN Architecture & BPTT, Recursive vs. Simple RNN, Deep/Multilayer & Bidirectional RNNs, LSTM, GRU, Encoder-Decoder RNNs, Language Modeling, Image Captioning & BERT

Module V: Autoencoders (Sparse, Denoising, Overcomplete, Stochastic, Variational/VAE), Boltzmann Machines, RBMs & Contrastive Divergence, Deep Belief Networks (DBNs), and GANs
